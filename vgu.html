About Vivekananda Global University Jaipur

Vivekananda Global University Jaipur is one of the top private institutions in Rajasthan. VGU was set up in 2012 and is supported by the Bagaria Education Trust. The university has revised its curriculum to match evolving career needs and is known across the country for industry-linked education.

The university has approvals from UGC, CoA, BCI, AICTE, PCI and membership of AIU. Its important departments include engineering and technology, basic and applied sciences, medical and pharmaceutical sciences, international trade and commerce, management, law, hospitality, architecture, design, journalism and mass communication, and humanities and social sciences.

VGU is described as a 4-star green campus in North India by ASSOCHAM rating and has hosted 500+ recruiters across sectors.

VGU University Admission

Vivekananda Global University takes admission at UG, PG, diploma and doctoral levels across many fields. Students can apply online and appear for VGU-CET or another applicable national entrance exam depending on the course.

  • Online Application: Students fill out the online application form and choose their preferred course.
  • Entrance Exams: VGU-CET, national entrance scores, DECODE for design, and AIFSET for forensic science may be considered for different programs.
  • Counselling: After merit declaration, applicants complete counselling, document verification and fee submission.
