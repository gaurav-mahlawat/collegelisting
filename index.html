About JECRC University Jaipur

JECRC University is one of the most sought-after private universities of Jaipur. The campus is close to Jaipur's industrial centre and main Sitapura institutional area, making it well connected to all parts of the Pink City.

JECRC Jaipur offers many courses and has set up a beautiful campus in Rajasthan’s capital city. The campus area is around 32 acres and is widely known for its distinctive landscaping and classical architecture.

Various entrances such as XAT, MAT, CAT, AIEE, JEE Mains, GATE, CLAT, Personal Interviews, and Group Discussions are considered for admission. Over 6k students study simultaneously at JECRC Jaipur, and it is especially famous for Engineering courses.

JECRC University Admissions

JECRC University admissions are done through online applications, entrance exam based admission, and lateral entry for eligible B.Tech students.

  • Online Mode: Students can check the university’s official website to know important dates and apply for their preferred course.
  • Entrance Exams Based Admission: Law, Management, and Engineering courses require relevant competitive exam scores such as JEE, CLAT, MAT, XAT, or CAT.
  • Lateral Entry: Diploma or B.Sc degree holders can apply for direct second-year entry into the B.Tech programme.
